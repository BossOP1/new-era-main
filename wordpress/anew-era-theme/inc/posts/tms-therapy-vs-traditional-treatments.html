Traditional Treatments: A Trusted Foundation

Medication and therapy have been the mainstay of treatment for depression and anxiety for many years. Medications can effectively regulate brain chemistry, while therapy equips you with tools to manage your symptoms. However, some individuals find that medications come with unwanted side effects or that therapy, while valuable, doesn’t provide the level of relief they need.

 TMS Therapy: A New Era of Treatment

TMS therapy offers a non-invasive approach that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate specific areas of the brain associated with mood regulation. This targeted approach can be highly effective for those without success with traditional treatments. Here’s how TMS compares:

  • Effectiveness: Studies show that TMS therapy can significantly improve symptoms in most patients, even those with treatment-resistant depression.
  • Fewer Side Effects: Compared to medications, TMS therapy boasts a milder side effect profile, typically limited to scalp discomfort or temporary headaches.
  • Non-invasive and Convenient: TMS therapy sessions are painless and require no anesthesia. You can resume your daily activities immediately after treatment.
