What is Pandemic Depression and how does it differ from other forms of depression?

The Covid-19 pandemic resulted in a lot of change and uncertainty in the world, and for many people was a very traumatic event leaving them with post pandemic stress disorder, post pandemic depression, and other mental health conditions.

Pandemic depression is a term that is used to describe the specific type of depression that can occur during or after a major global event such as a public health crisis. This type of depression is often characterized by symptoms such as feelings of sadness, hopelessness, and difficulty concentrating or sleeping.
