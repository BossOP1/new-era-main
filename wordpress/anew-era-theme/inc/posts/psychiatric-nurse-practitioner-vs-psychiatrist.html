Comparing Psychiatric Nurse Practitioners to Psychiatrists

Comparing the qualifications, scope of practice, and cost associated with each type of provider can help you make the best choice for your mental health care.

What is a Psychiatric Nurse Practitioner?

A Psychiatric Nurse Practitioner (PNP) is a master’s or doctorally-prepared nurse with advanced education in mental health care and diagnosis. PNPs have nursing degrees, advanced practice nursing, and specialize in diagnosing, treating, and managing psychiatric illnesses such as depression, anxiety disorders, eating disorders, bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, and other mental health disorders. They are authorized to prescribe medication, provide psychotherapy and conduct diagnostic exams.

What is a Psychiatrist?

A Psychiatrist is a medical doctor who specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of mental health issues. Unlike PNPs, Psychiatrists have completed medical school and must be licensed by their state to practice medicine. They are also able to order lab tests, perform physical exams and prescribe medications.

In addition to being experts in diagnosing and treating mental illnesses, they can also work with patients to develop coping strategies. This may help reduce symptoms or improve moods.

According to the Bureau of Labor Statistics, a psychiatrist’s annual salary averages higher than that of a psychiatric nurse practitioner.

The Key Differences Between PNPs and Psychiatrists

Although there are some similarities between Psychiatric Nurse Practitioners (PNPs) and Psychiatrists, they also have important differences that should be considered when selecting a provider.

The primary difference between a Psychiatric Nurse Practitioner and a Psychiatrist is their educational requirements.

Another major difference is the length of training required for each type of professional. PNPs undergo special training that is shorter than the medical school and residency required for Psychiatrists.

In addition, PNPs are able to provide psychotherapy, while Psychiatrists may take a more medical approach to treatment for psychiatric disorders.
