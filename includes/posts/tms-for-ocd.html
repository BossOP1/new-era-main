Does TMS Work for OCD?

Obsessive Compulsive Disorder (OCD) is a mental health disorder characterized by obsessions and compulsions. Obsessions are recurrent and intrusive thoughts, images, or impulses that cause significant distress and interfere with daily functioning. Compulsions are behaviors or mental rituals an individual performs to reduce anxiety related to obsessions. Common examples of OCD include fear of germs or contamination, fear of harm to oneself or others, hoarding, and need for symmetry or order.

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is an FDA-approved treatment for OCD that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate areas of the brain associated with OCD. Research has shown that it can reduce symptoms in people with OCD. A large meta-analysis of 15 randomized controlled trials found that TMS had a significant effect on decreasing symptoms in over 70% of individuals with OCD.

The effects of using TMS to treat OCD are positive and long-lasting. Research has shown that the benefits remain for up to several months after the initial course of treatment and that the effects of TMS can be cumulative. Treatment using TMS is non-invasive and has few side effects, making it a safe and effective treatment option for OCD.

Using TMS to treat OCD can have a significant clinical benefit. Studies have found that people who received treatment with TMS had significantly greater improvements in their symptoms compared to those who received a placebo or no treatment.

Other treatments may be beneficial alongside TMS Therapy for OCD treatment. Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) is an effective approach to treating OCD and can be combined with medication and/or TMS therapy.

When attending a TMS session, you should expect the treatment to last approximately 45 minutes per session. During the session, a doctor will use a magnetic coil to deliver magnetic pulses to specific areas of your brain associated with OCD. You may experience some mild discomfort during the procedure, but it should be tolerable and considered safe. The number of treatment sessions can vary depending on the individual’s response to treatment but typically ranges from 20-40 treatments before results are seen.

Overall, TMS and deep TMS is an effective and safe treatment option for individuals with OCD that can provide long-lasting relief from symptoms. With the help of a knowledgeable healthcare provider, TMS can be used to successfully manage OCD symptoms.
